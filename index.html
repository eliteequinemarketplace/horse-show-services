Horse Braiders for Hunter Jumper Shows

Professional braiding is a highly skilled craft that significantly affects a hunter's presentation in the ring — and finding a reliable, skilled braider who is available at the right shows and times is one of the most competitive resource challenges on the circuit. Top braiders at WEF, WEC, and the major circuits book up quickly during peak season. Elite Equine Marketplace lists professional braiders serving the major hunter jumper show circuits, allowing riders and trainers to book ahead and ensure consistent presentation throughout the season.

Body Clipping for Competition

Proper body clipping is essential for competition horses — maintaining a clean, well-presented appearance that reflects the quality of the horse and the program. Many trainers prefer to use professional clip services rather than clip horses in-house, particularly for full body clips and complex clip patterns. Elite Equine Marketplace lists professional clip services from experienced equine clip professionals serving barns and show circuits nationwide.
